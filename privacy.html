Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Syme Skyworks is a small business in Canmore, Alberta, operated by Malcolm Syme. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why, and how we protect it. We follow Alberta's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us:

  • Contact form: your name, email address, the service you need, and your message.
  • Snow shoveling and landscaping sign-up form: your name, phone number, email address, house number and street, the service details you choose, and any notes.
  • Email or phone: anything you include when you contact us directly.

This website has no accounts, advertising, or tracking or analytics tools, and it does not set cookies. To remember that you closed the seasonal pop-up, your own browser stores a small note on your device. It is never sent to us.

Why we use it

We use your information only to reply to you, give quotes, schedule and provide the services you ask for, send invoices, and keep basic business records. We do not sell, rent or trade your information, and we do not use it for marketing unless you ask us to.

Consent

By sending a form or contacting us, you consent to us using your information for these purposes. You can withdraw your consent at any time by emailing us. We will then stop using your information, except where the law requires us to keep records.

Who else handles it

  • Formspree delivers the website's forms to our email. Its servers may be outside Canada, including in the United States, where your information may be subject to that country's laws.
  • Google (Gmail) hosts our email.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website, and OpenStreetMap supplies the map images. As with any website, these services may record technical details such as your IP address when your browser loads the page.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it to provide our services. We delete sign-up details when you stop using our services or ask us to. Invoices and payment records are kept as long as the law requires, generally six years for tax purposes.

How we protect it

We use reasonable safeguards, including password-protected accounts with two-step verification where available, and we limit access to Malcolm Syme only.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email mcsyme11@gmail.com and we will respond within 45 days.

Questions or concerns

Contact Malcolm Syme at mcsyme11@gmail.com or +1 (403) 679-1495. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new effective date.
