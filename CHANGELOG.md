# Changelog

## 2026-11-23 · Snow shoveling and landscaping

### Added
- Snow Shoveling & Landscaping section (`#seasonal`): tabs, service-area map with a 600 m ring around the neighbourhood, and a sign-up / pre-season registration form with a street list.
- Seasonal pop-up: snow shoveling (November–March) and landscaping (April–October), shown once per visitor per season.
- Service cards, two seasonal pricing cards (snow from $16 per visit; landscaping free quote) and two FAQ answers.
- Leaflet 1.9.4 map library in `assets/vendor/leaflet/` (served from the site, no outside script host); OpenStreetMap tiles, dimmed to match the site.

### Changed
- Menu: added "Snow & Yards"; FAQ moved to the footer menu; compact menu now starts below 1000 px wide so it never overflows.
- Section backgrounds re-alternated around the new section.

## 2026-11-20 · Branding and copyright on every file

### Added
- Mission patch at the top, and the standard © and trademark footer at the bottom, of every document (changelog, design guide, history, repo rules, logo guide, hand-off).
- Copyright written into the file details of every photo, icon and screenshot (36 images; pictures unchanged).
- Visible © and trademark line on the 404 page.
- Credit in `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml` and `site.webmanifest`.
- `brand/social-preview.png`: branded 1280 × 640 card for GitHub link previews.

## 2026-11-17 · Published, and repo filled in

### Added
- Site published on GitHub Pages: https://mcsyme11-byte.github.io/SymeSkyWorks/
- `404.html` "Off the flight plan" page, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `.nojekyll`.
- Canonical link, full share-image address and business details for search engines.
- Professional `README.md` with screenshots, `LICENSE` (all rights reserved) and `CLAUDE.md` repo rules.

### Changed
- Founding year 2023 confirmed by Malcolm.

## 2026-11-17 · Website v2 and new logo

### Added
- Mission patch adopted as the main logo (hero, footer and share image).
- `brand/mark/`: simplified mark without ring text (32–64 px) and a small favicon mark (under 32 px).
- Favicons (`favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`), Apple touch icon, 192/512 px app icons and `site.webmanifest`.
- Gallery viewer (select a photo to enlarge it).
- Phone number in Contact; service dropdown in the form.
- `README.md`, `brand/README.md`, `docs/DESIGN.md`, `docs/HISTORY.md`, this changelog.
- Credit and trademark lines in the footer.

### Changed
- Full redesign in the shared navy, brass and cream look with Georgia serif type.
- Photos moved from inside `index.html` into `assets/img/` with 720 px thumbnails; page size down from about 4.2 MB.
- Emoji icons replaced with original line icons.
- Section order: Services, Drone, Construction, Gallery, Pricing, About, FAQ, Contact.
- "Est. 2024" corrected to "Est. 2023".

### Removed
- Old text logo, blue colour scheme and Bebas Neue / DM fonts.
- Custom cursor, aurora, shooting stars and scroll progress bar.
- Street address from the public page.

The contact form endpoint (Formspree) is unchanged.
